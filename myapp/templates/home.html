What services does Pranik Medical Services (PMS) offer? How do I access PMS?

You can access PMS through our website. PMS offers a wide range of healthcare services, including online appointments, medical advice, prescription management, and health monitoring.

Can I book appointments with Local Clinic doctors through PMS?

Yes, you can easily book appointments with doctors of your choice through our platform. We allow online clinic appointment scheduling, enabling you to find nearby clinics, view their availability, and book appointments from anywhere.

Are there any fees associated with using PMS?

The fees for using PMS are only 2 Rs per appointment, and other services such as our AI chatbot for meal and medicine recommendations are completely free.

Is my data safe? Are there any PMS age limits?

Yes, we prioritize the security of your personal information and have robust measures in place to protect it. PMS is open to individuals of all age groups, ensuring access to healthcare services for everyone.

What should I do if I experience technical difficulties on PMS?

If you encounter any technical issues, please reach out to our support team for prompt assistance.
