India has over 180 million arthritis patients, yet 60% remain undiagnosed until significant joint damage occurs. The Indian Rheumatology Association's new guidelines emphasize early intervention, especially for the unique challenges faced in tropical climates.

Monsoon Woes

Humidity changes dramatically affect joint symptoms. Mumbai clinics report 40% increased flare-ups during monsoon. Key management strategies:

"Many patients believe arthritis is inevitable with aging. But with modern treatments, 80% can maintain full function if diagnosed early." - Dr. Amit Patel, President, Indian Rheumatology Association

New Biologics Access Program

Indian-made biosimilars have reduced costs by 70%. Key developments:

  • Patient Assistance: Many manufacturers offer income-based pricing
  • Tele-rheumatology: Rural patients can access specialists
  • Home Infusion: Trained nurses administer biologics at home
